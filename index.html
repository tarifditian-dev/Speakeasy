French for Immigration

TEF Canada Preparation

Your French Ticket to Permanent Residency in Canada

The TEF Canada (Test d'Évaluation de Français) is an official language proficiency exam recognised by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). A strong TEF Canada score directly impacts your Express Entry CRS points — and can be the difference between an invitation to apply and waiting indefinitely. At Speakeasy, we prepare you to achieve CLB 7 or CLB 8+ with structured coaching, mock tests, and expert feedback.

🍁 TEF Canada IRCC Recognised
🎧
Compréhension Orale

Listening Comprehension — audio recordings, dialogues, announcements

📖
Compréhension Écrite

Reading Comprehension — texts, articles, notices, emails

✍️
Expression Écrite

Written Expression — two written tasks, formal & informal registers

🎙️
Expression Orale

Oral Expression — recorded responses on given topics and situations

CLB Score Breakdown — What You Need to Know
🇨🇦 Canadian Language Benchmark
CLB 7 Intermediate

CLB 7 — Intermediate Advanced

The baseline score required for Federal Skilled Worker (FSW) and most Express Entry programs. Achieving CLB 7 in all four skills adds significant CRS points to your profile.

✅ Express Entry Eligible
🎧 Listening
217 – 248
📖 Reading
206 – 232
✍️ Writing
226 – 270
🎙️ Speaking
226 – 270
What it means for CRS: CLB 7 in French as a second official language adds up to 24 bonus CRS points (FSW) and qualifies you for French-language draws with lower cutoff scores. Ideal for candidates with B1–B2 French level.
🇨🇦 Canadian Language Benchmark
CLB 8 Advanced

CLB 8 — Upper Advanced

The gold-standard target for maximum CRS points. CLB 8 in all four TEF Canada components unlocks the highest French-language bonus and dramatically boosts your overall Express Entry score.

🏆 Maximum CRS Boost
🎧 Listening
249 – 279
📖 Reading
233 – 247
✍️ Writing
271 – 309
🎙️ Speaking
271 – 309
What it means for CRS: CLB 8 in French as a second language can add up to 50+ bonus CRS points, making it one of the single most impactful actions for an Express Entry candidate. Recommended for B2–C1 French learners.
